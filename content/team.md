I mentor student researchers through ANNT's _Projekat studentskih istraživanja_ (PSI), a programme connecting students with researchers on focused research projects.


## AI-Assisted Formal Verification of Robotic Control Systems {#ai-assisted-formal-verification-of-robotic-control-systems}

2026 · ANNT PSI

We are building a Lean 4 library of machine-checked proofs for control theory and robotics, covering stability, controllability, manipulator kinematics, and safety invariants. We also evaluate whether AI assistants can shorten the time needed to produce proofs accepted by Lean 4.

## Extremum Seeking MPPT Against Commercial Power Optimizers {#extremum-seeking-mppt-against-commercial-power-optimizers}

2026 · ANNT PSI

We investigate extremum seeking for maximum power point tracking (MPPT) in solar panel arrays. The project combines controller implementation and hardware experiments under partial shading and changing conditions, with comparisons against commercial optimizer specifications and field measurements. We will assess convergence time, steady-state error, transient behaviour, and cost.
