I am an assistant professor at the International University of Sarajevo. My research background is in control and optimization. I also lead scientific initiatives through the Association for the Advancement of Science and Technology.


## Education {#education}

-   **2019–2023** — PhD, Delft University of Technology. Thesis: _Derivative-free equilibrium seeking in multi-agent systems_.
-   **2016–2018** — MSc in Electrical Engineering, Automatic Control and Electronics, University of Sarajevo.
-   **2013–2016** — BSc in Electrical Engineering, Automatic Control and Electronics, University of Sarajevo.


## Experience {#experience}
